### Question 310 — Ministries
**🇩🇪** Welche Senatorin/welchen Senator hat Berlin nicht?
**🇬🇧** Which Senator does Berlin NOT have?

| | Deutsch | English |
|---|---|---|
| ○ | Finanzsenator/Finanzsenatorin | Finance Senator |
| ○ | Innensenator/Innensenatorin | Interior Senator |
| ✅ | **Senator/Senatorin für Außenbeziehungen** | **Senator for External Affairs** |
| ○ | Justizsenator/Justizsenatorin | Justice Senator |

> **📝 Explanation:** Foreign affairs are a federal responsibility in Germany, not a state matter — so no German state has this ministry.
