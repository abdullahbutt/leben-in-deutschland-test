Saxony (Sachsen) is located in eastern Germany, bordering Poland and the Czech Republic. Its capital is Dresden, home to the Landtag and renowned for its Baroque architecture and world-class art collections (Zwinger, Semperoper). Leipzig is another major city, famous as the home of Bach and the site of the 1989 Monday demonstrations that helped bring down the Berlin Wall. The head of government is the Ministerpräsident/in.
