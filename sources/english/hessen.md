| ○ | 16 | 16 |

> **📝 Explanation:** In Hesse, the minimum voting age for municipal elections is 18 — unlike some other German states, which allow voting from age 16 at the local level.

### Question 309 — Head of Government
**🇩🇪** Wie nennt man den Regierungschef / die Regierungschefin in Hessen?
**🇬🇧** What is the head of government in Hesse called?

| | Deutsch | English |
|---|---|---|
| ○ | Erster Minister / Erste Ministerin | First Minister |
| ○ | Premierminister / Premierministerin | Prime Minister |
| ○ | Bürgermeister / Bürgermeisterin | Mayor |
| ✅ | **Ministerpräsident / Ministerpräsidentin** | **Minister-President** |

> **📝 Explanation:** As in all German states, the head of government in Hesse holds the title Ministerpräsident/in (Minister-President) — not Prime Minister or Mayor. As of 2025: Boris Rhein (CDU).

### Question 310 — Ministries
**🇩🇪** Welchen Minister / welche Ministerin hat Hessen nicht?
**🇬🇧** Which minister does Hesse NOT have?

| | Deutsch | English |
|---|---|---|
| ○ | Justizminister / Justizministerin | Minister of Justice |
| ✅ | **Außenminister / Außenministerin** | **Minister of Foreign Affairs** |
| ○ | Finanzminister / Finanzministerin | Minister of Finance |
| ○ | Innenminister / Innenministerin | Minister of the Interior |

> **📝 Explanation:** Foreign affairs are a federal responsibility in Germany, not a state matter — so no German state, including Hesse, has a Minister of Foreign Affairs.

### Additional State Facts
Key facts for your preparation:
- **Parliament:** Landtag
- **Neighbors/Borders:** No international borders. Borders: Niedersachsen, Thüringen, Bayern, Baden-Württemberg, Rheinland-Pfalz, NRW
- **Major cities:** Frankfurt am Main, Wiesbaden, Kassel, Darmstadt, Offenbach
